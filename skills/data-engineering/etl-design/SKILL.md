# Etl Design

Use when design extract/transform/load jobs and quality gates.

## Procedure

1. Map sources and targets.
2. Draft transformations and schedules.
3. Check nullability and deduplication.

## Verification

- Validate with data quality tests.
- No duplicate or partial load.
- Check idempotency.
