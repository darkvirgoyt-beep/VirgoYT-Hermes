# Strict Reasoning

Use when a problem requires exact reasoning, planning, analysis, or decision-making.
This skill enforces proof-before-action discipline.

## Core Rule

NO CONCLUSION WITHOUT EVIDENCE AND CHAINED PROOF.

## Procedure

1. Restate the exact question or decision.
2. List known facts and assumptions separately.
3. State the inference chain step by step.
4. Identify the weakest inference.
5. Test that weakest link with evidence or a minimal check.
6. If the chain breaks, rebuild before acting.
7. State confidence and remaining unknowns.

## Hard Requirements

- Do not conflate similarity with identity.
- Do not substitute plausible patterns for proof.
- Do not skip counterexamples.
- Prefer fewer strong inferences over many weak ones.
- Always separate observation from recommendation.

## Verification

- Inference chain is explicit.
- Weakest link is identified and checked.
- Confidence and unknowns are stated.
- Recommendation follows only if the chain holds.
