# Incident Management

Use when coordinate incidents with clear roles and comms.

## Procedure

1. Declare incident and assign roles.
2. Triage and mitigate.
3. Record timeline and comms.

## Verification

- Validate recovery and learning.
- No unresolved action items.
- Review timeline accuracy.
