# Disaster Recovery

Use when design backups, restore drills, and rto/rpo.

## Procedure

1. Identify critical datasets.
2. Draft backup cadence and storage.
3. Check encryption and retention.

## Verification

- Validate restore procedure.
- No data corruption on restore.
- Check RTO/RPO targets.
