# Secrets Management

Use when scan, rotate, and store secrets safely.

## Procedure

1. Identify secret stores.
2. Scan repos for leaked keys.
3. Check rotation and access logs.

## Verification

- Verify least-privilege access.
- No leaked credentials.
- Audit access and rotation.
